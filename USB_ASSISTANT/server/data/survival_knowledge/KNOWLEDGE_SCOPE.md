# Knowledge Scope

## Purpose
Define what topics the Survival Companion should be able to answer questions about.

## Core Topics

### Emergency Preparedness
- Disaster planning and response
- FEMA guidelines
- Nuclear/EMP scenarios
- Evacuation planning
- Emergency kits and checklists

### Wilderness Survival
- Shelter building
- Fire starting
- Water sourcing and purification
- Navigation (map, compass, natural signs)
- Signaling for rescue

### Food & Foraging
- Edible plants (general guidance)
- Trapping basics
- Fishing techniques
- Food preservation
- Rationing and storage

### First Aid
- Wound care
- Burns and blisters
- Fractures and sprains
- Hypothermia/hyperthermia
- Common illness treatment

### Off-Grid Living
- Solar power basics
- Water harvesting
- Gardening and agriculture
- Food preservation
- Waste management

### Communications
- Ham radio basics
- Mesh networks (Meshtastic, LoRa)
- Signal mirrors and flags
- Emergency frequencies

### Security
- Situational awareness
- Home hardening
- Basic self-defense concepts

## Source Materials (Public Domain / Open)

### Military Manuals
- FM 21-76 (US Army Survival)
- USMC survival courses
- Cold weather training guides

### Government Resources
- FEMA emergency guides
- CDC preparedness materials
- EPA water safety

### Open Resources
- [TruePrepper PDF collection](https://trueprepper.com/survival-pdfs-downloads/)
- [Awesome Survival repo](https://github.com/alx-xlx/awesome-survival)
- Historical texts (1911 Boy Scout Handbook, etc.)

## Out of Scope
- Detailed medical diagnosis
- Legal advice
- Weapons manufacturing
- Specific drug dosages
